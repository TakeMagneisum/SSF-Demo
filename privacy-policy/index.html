How information is handled

Privacy policy

This page explains the information involved in using SSF. It describes the current static website and email routing; it is not a guarantee that internet infrastructure processes no technical data.

Effective November 15, 2026. This policy describes the supplied website build and the email setup reported by its operator. If technologies change, the disclosures should be reviewed and updated.

Browsing and local features

SSF does not ask visitors to create an account, run advertising pixels, or intentionally profile browsing behavior. Site content is delivered through GitHub Pages, using Cloudflare for domain DNS and associated services. Hosting, DNS, network, and security providers may process routine technical request information such as IP addresses, timestamps, and browser/network metadata under their own policies.

Appearance, reading settings, Calm View and language may be saved in browser local storage. Selected guide and country are separate local records. The finder also remembers selected resource IDs in this tab’s session storage when possible; use Clear sheet in the sheet review to clear them. Reset display settings removes the display-preferences record only; it does not clear language, Calm View, selected guides, browser history or handouts. Display preferences are not sent to SSF. Dawn’s deterministic on-page guide search operates locally; SSF does not submit the situation text to a service, store it in application storage, or include it in generated PDF sheets. Browser history, form restoration, caches, clipboard, downloaded files and printed copies are outside these controls. Opening external links or scanning QR codes sends a request to the destination. A shared or monitored device may retain evidence.

This only removes the saved guide and country, not the address of an already open page, handouts, display settings or browser history. Opening another guide can remember it again.

Optional ZIP codes

ZIP entry is optional. SSF can place a submitted ZIP in the discovery page URL to show prepared searches and potential nearby guides. This means the ZIP may appear in browser history, shared links, and technical requests to the website host. If you choose an external prepared-search link, its destination may receive the ZIP or search terms. ZIP codes do not prove residence, jurisdiction, or eligibility. You can instead browse topics without a ZIP.

Emails and corrections

Messages sent to contact@singlestepforward.com and feedback@singlestepforward.com are routed through Cloudflare Email Routing to a Proton Mail inbox accessible to the project operator. An email can contain your sender address, message, attachments, and other mail metadata. No fixed deletion schedule is currently promised. Messages may be retained for correspondence, source verification, or project records until deleted.

Do not send account credentials, identification documents, medical histories, private case details, or information about other people without authorization. Requests about your emails can be directed to the contact address; legal rights and practical deletion limits depend on the circumstances.

External websites and contributions

Official agency links, the American Heart Association, American Red Cross, and other external resources have their own data practices. SSF does not control those services. The optional Ko-fi contribution link takes you to a separate website. Ko-fi and the payment provider selected at checkout handle payment information under their own privacy policies. SSF does not embed a payment form or collect payment card details through this website.

Updates and contact

If SSF adds a hosted form, email automation, analytics, AI processing, or on-site payment features, this policy must be updated to describe the real implementation. Questions: contact@singlestepforward.com.
