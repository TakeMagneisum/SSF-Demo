Accessibility settings

Adjust this website without changing your browser settings. You can still use browser zoom.

Contact & website support

Need help using this website, or have a question about how SSF works? These contacts are for the website itself. To apply for services, contact the organization listed in a guide.

Need assistance now? SSF does not award financial aid, provide case management, handle eligibility decisions, or monitor emergencies. Browse types of help or view reviewed local guides. Call 911 for immediate danger in the U.S.; call or text 988 for U.S. mental health or suicide crises.

Feedback & corrections

Report an incorrect or missing resource

For broken links, incorrect program details, outdated information, missing services, or suggestions:

feedback@singlestepforward.com

  • The relevant public webpage URL and resource name
  • What seems incorrect or unclear
  • An official source or reference, if you have one

Please do not send Social Security numbers, banking details, identity documents, medical records, or anyone else’s personal information.

What happens to an email?

Messages are forwarded using Cloudflare Email Routing to the project operator’s existing Proton Mail inbox. These addresses are not continuously monitored, and individual replies or resolution times are not guaranteed. Public feedback may inform future reviews, but a report is not treated as verified until checked.
